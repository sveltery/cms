The currently inherited draft-only table cannot hold the source's published First/Second Post fixtures, and typed transport alone does not add lifecycle behavior.

A test-only trusted-session fixture may establish initial transport/interaction failures but cannot replace that final evidence. Rich widgets and later administrative features have separate owners. No current source inventory establishes a completed or usable full product.
