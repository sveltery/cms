# Compact Calendar keyboard preparation

This private candidate keeps the compact Calendar's focused day separate from its chosen day. Arrow keys move by a day or week. Shift with horizontal arrows moves by a month, and Shift with vertical arrows moves by a year. Page Up/Down move by a month, or a year with Shift. Home/End use the existing date-fns locale's week boundaries. Enter and Space select through the actual native button. Month and year moves clamp to the destination month's final day, using the Source Calendar's local noon conversion across daylight saving changes. Active focus and last focus remain separate; returning to a month restores its available last-focused tab target without automatically moving focus from the toolbar.
